Cyclops Story

It all started in Dith's garden. A one-eyed stray appeared like a glitch in the matrix: scrappy, silent, and somehow staring straight into your soul.

Dith's wife said he stays. Dith agreed on one condition: he gets to name him. Thus, Cyclops was born.

Now he is part of the chain. One eye. Nine lives. Infinite possibilities. Welcome to the cult of Cyclops.
